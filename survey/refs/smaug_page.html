Limited Edtion Mighty Smaug The Golden

December 9, 2014December 9, 2014| DracDrac| 2 Comments| 9:05 am
Categories:
  • Beyond LOTRO
  • Games

Behold! Smaug The Golden! There’s only 200 of them, This miniature from Games Workshop. It’s designed for use with The Hobbit Strategy Battle Game.

“This magnificent 42 piece miniature is hand cast using the highest quality resin (the same used by Forge World), and the most advanced techniques to produce a model with extremely fine detail. Every scale, horn and battle-scar has been expertly reproduced to create a beatifically authentic collectors piece.”

 

The miniature is called Mighty Smaug The Golden. Weighing in at 2.4kgs, the height of the miniature from the bottom of the base to the tip of Smaug’s™ wing is 8.4 inches (or 21.4 cm). The base is 9.2 inches (or 23.1 cm) long and 5.4 inches (or 13.3 cm) wide.

The 200-limit is “this side of Christmas” so there might be more next year.
